What this money will not do

Every program has strings. Here are these, so nobody finds out at the closing table.

It never touches your hands. Your lender applies for it and the city sends it to the closing table.
You have to live there. It has to be your main home. Sell, move out or refinance out early and repayment rules kick in.
It is for first-time buyers. That means no home owned in the last 3 years. If you only owned with a spouse and you are divorced now, you may still count.
It will not get you a loan. You still have to qualify with a lender on your own credit and income.
You have to sit the class. An 8-hour HUD-approved homebuyer class, certificate in hand, before you close. Each buyer signs up separately.
It is not a sure thing. Meeting the rules is not the same as the city having money left this year.
Step 3

What else can sit on top

These are separate programs. Some pay you up front, some pay you back later. The difference matters.

Facade grant
$15,000
Cash up front
For the outside of the house. This one pays first, unlike the rest. Ask the city about the income limit, it is not posted clearly.
Repair rebate
40%
Paid back later Call to confirm
The city pays back 40% of approved repair work, minimum $10,000 of work, at or below 120% of area median income. You pay the contractor first, then the check comes after inspection. Never money at closing. The program turns on and off, so call before you count on it.
Bank grants
$8,000–$15,000
Bank of America and Fifth Third had grants for this area as of June 2026. They can sit on top of the city money. Ask the lender what is open right now.
FHA 203(k) repair loan
One loan that buys the house and pays for the repairs, with the repair money held by the lender and released as work is finished. It is a loan, so you pay it back. Stacking it with the city money should work, but few lenders have done it. Confirm with both the lender and the city first.

Who does what

The city
Runs the program and holds the money.
Your lender
Files the application for you. You do not apply on your own. Find one who has closed these before.
Neighborhood Home Solutions
The class and free counseling. That is all. They do not approve or fund anything.
