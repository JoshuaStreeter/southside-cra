I make $70K — do I qualify?

For a two-person household, $70,000 sits above the 80% line and between 81% and 120% of area median income, so you may qualify on income. Household size moves that line, so tell me how many people will live there and I can be exact.

One thing to know: as of April 2026 the city was over its spending limit for that band, so applications there may sit on a waitlist. Ask where funding stands before you write an offer.
